# Orbitly Refund & Cancellation Policy

## Cancelling your subscription
You can cancel at any time from Billing Settings > Subscription > Cancel Plan. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period - you keep full access until then, and you will not be charged again after that date.

## Refunds for monthly plans
Monthly subscriptions are not refunded for partial months. If you cancel mid-cycle, you retain access until the cycle ends but do not receive a prorated refund for the unused portion.

## Refunds for annual plans
Annual subscriptions can be refunded on a prorated basis within the first 30 days of the annual term only. After 30 days, annual plans follow the same no-refund-for-unused-time policy as monthly plans, but you keep access through the end of the paid year.

## Downgrades
Downgrading to a lower plan takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle, not immediately. If your current usage (team members, storage, active projects) exceeds the lower plan's limits, you will be prompted to reduce usage before the downgrade can complete.

## Accidental duplicate charges
If you are charged twice for the same billing period due to a payment processing error, contact support with your invoice numbers. Duplicate charges are refunded in full, typically within 5-7 business days, and do not require a support ticket escalation.

## Data retention after cancellation
Account data is retained for 90 days after cancellation in case you want to reactivate. After 90 days, all project data, files, and client portal content are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. You can export your data as a ZIP archive at any time before the 90-day window closes, from Settings > Export Data.

## Chargebacks
Initiating a chargeback with your card issuer instead of contacting support directly will result in immediate account suspension while the dispute is investigated, per our terms of service.

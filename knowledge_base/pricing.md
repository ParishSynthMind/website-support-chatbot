# Orbitly Pricing & Plans

Orbitly is a project-scheduling platform for small agencies. We offer three plans, billed monthly or annually.

## Starter - $19/month (or $190/year)
- Up to 5 team members
- 10 active projects
- 2GB file storage
- Email support (48-hour response time)
- Core scheduling and task boards

## Growth - $49/month (or $490/year)
- Up to 20 team members
- Unlimited active projects
- 50GB file storage
- Priority email support (12-hour response time)
- Everything in Starter, plus: time tracking, client portals, custom branding

## Scale - $129/month (or $1,290/year)
- Unlimited team members
- Unlimited active projects
- 500GB file storage
- Dedicated support with a named account manager, live chat included
- Everything in Growth, plus: SSO/SAML, audit logs, API access, custom roles and permissions

## Annual billing discount
Paying annually saves approximately 17% compared to monthly billing on every plan (two months free).

## Add-ons (available on any plan)
- Extra storage: $10/month per additional 50GB
- Extra team seats beyond plan limit: $6/month per seat (Starter and Growth only; Scale is unlimited)

## Free trial
All plans include a 14-day free trial, no credit card required. Trials automatically downgrade to a limited free tier (1 project, 2 team members) if not upgraded, rather than being deleted.

## Nonprofit discount
Registered nonprofits receive 30% off any plan, verified annually through our nonprofit verification partner. Apply via the billing settings page.
